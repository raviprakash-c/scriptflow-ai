## 2. Procedure

1. Record the campaign brief, target audience, objective, topic, tone, platforms and dates.
2. Generate sample drafts in the local Social Content Studio workspace.
3. Save the exact generated copy for each variant before editing.
4. Apply the planned single-variable change. Keep all other conditions as consistent as practical.
5. Have a reviewer assess each draft against the same written rubric; record reviewer and date.
6. If the experiment involves real distribution, record the actual platform post URL and the platform's native analytics export. Do not treat the local planning calendar as publication evidence.
7. Record raw observations, missing data, deviations and limitations before calculating any summary.
8. Repeat only as specified by the official brief; do not claim statistical significance without a suitable design and sample size.

## Current product limitations to disclose

Social Content Studio currently uses local browser storage and a deterministic sample/template generator. Social OAuth, connected accounts, external scheduling, and real publishing are not implemented. The experiment can assess the draft/review/planning workflow locally; it cannot by itself prove real-world marketing performance.
